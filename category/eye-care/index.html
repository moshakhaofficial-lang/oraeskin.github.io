How to Choose the Right Under-Eye Treatments for Indian Weather

In India's tropical and subtropical climate, choosing under-eye treatments requires accounting for high humidity, sweat, strong UV radiation, and environmental pollution.

  • Match Formula to Season: In hot humid monsoons, choose lightweight, oil-free gel or water-burst textures that absorb without leaving a suffocating film. During cooler winter months, opt for barrier-replenishing ceramide formulas.
  • Check Active Stability: Certain active ingredients (such as raw L-Ascorbic Acid) degrade quickly in Indian ambient heat. We recommend stabilized derivatives like Ethyl Ascorbic Acid, encapsulated Retinol, and photostable UV filters.
  • Patch Test Before Full Application: When introducing potent actives, always patch test on the jawline or inner forearm for 24 hours to monitor for sensitivity.
