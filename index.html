🛡️ Routine Safety & Active Introduction Guidelines

  • Patch Test First: Apply a dime-sized amount of new active treatments behind your ear or inner arm 24 hours before facial use.
  • Introduce Actives Gradually: Begin using corrective serums 2–3 times a week before increasing to nightly application.
  • Never Skip Daily Sunscreen: Active treatments (especially exfoliating acids and retinoids) increase skin photosensitivity. Broad-spectrum SPF is non-negotiable.
  • Dermatology Medical Notice: Skincare guidance on OraeSkin is for educational purposes. If you suffer from cystic or persistent severe acne, consult a board-certified dermatologist.
