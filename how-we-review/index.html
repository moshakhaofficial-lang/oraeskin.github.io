How We Review & Score Skincare

Evidence-informed, climate-aware, and completely independent. Here is exactly how every product enters our database and earns its OraeSkin Score.

📋 Published: September 2026 ⚖️ Zero Sponsored Rankings 🇮🇳 Built for Indian Skin & Climate

Our Transparency Commitment

OraeSkin is an independent skincare research and editorial publication. We do not claim to operate a private clinical testing laboratory, nor do we employ fictional "celebrity dermatologists" to rubber-stamp commercial products.

Instead, we evaluate skincare through rigorous formulation analysis, peer-reviewed dermatological literature, real-world Indian climatic factors, verified purchaser sentiment, and strict price-per-millilitre economics.

1. How Products Enter Our Radar

With thousands of skincare launches hitting Indian marketplaces every month, we filter aggressively before a product is selected for deep evaluation. A formula must satisfy several baseline criteria:

🇮🇳

Indian Availability

Must be officially available and reliably restocked on Amazon India, official brand portals, or Indian pharmacies at standard retail prices.

🔬

Formulation Transparency

We prioritize brands that disclose full ingredient lists (INCI) and declare active concentrations (e.g., Niacinamide 5%, Salicylic Acid 2%).

💬

Verified Indian Consumer Proof

We analyze hundreds of verified Indian consumer reviews across heat, monsoon, and dry winter seasons to spot breakout trends or packaging flaws.

💰

Price-to-Value Accessibility

We look for formulations that deliver maximum barrier support or corrective efficacy within accessible Indian budgets (₹250 – ₹1,200).

2. The OraeSkin Score (Out of 10)

Every product on OraeSkin receives our proprietary OraeSkin Score. Unlike marketplace star ratings which often reflect delivery speed or unboxing excitement, our score is a multi-dimensional editorial assessment weighted across six key pillars:

1. Formula & Active Ingredients 25% Weight

Quality, stability, and concentration of hero actives (e.g., pure L-Ascorbic Acid vs ethylated derivatives, encapsulated Salicylic Acid, ceramide ratios). Penalty deductions for unnecessary drying alcohols or aggressive synthetic dyes.

2. Skin-Type & Climate Suitability 20% Weight

How the vehicle behaves in Indian conditions: 38°C summers, 85% monsoon humidity, sweat resistance, absence of white cast on Fitzpatrick skin types III–VI, and comedogenic potential in polluted metros.

3. Scientific Evidence Quality 20% Weight

We cross-reference active ingredients against PubMed literature, Cochrane reviews, and dermatological clinical consensus. We reward ingredients backed by double-blind randomized trials over fleeting wellness trends.

4. Verified User Sentiment 15% Weight

Systematic synthesis of verified purchaser feedback. We identify recurring real-world issues: pilling under makeup, eye stinging, batch inconsistencies, or pump dispenser jams.

5. Value for Money & Price-Per-ml 10% Weight

Calculation of cost per unit volume (₹/ml or ₹/g) based on recommended daily dosing. A ₹500 sunscreen for 50ml costs ₹10/ml, whereas a ₹799 formula for 100g costs ₹8/g. We evaluate economic sustainability for daily use.

6. Availability & Brand Reliability 10% Weight

Consistent marketplace availability, tamper-evident packaging, shelf-life stability, batch traceability, and adherence to Indian cosmetic regulatory standards.

3. Evidence Quality Classification

When evaluating claims made on packaging or marketing campaigns, we classify active ingredients using a 4-tier evidentiary standard:

Evidence Tier Criteria & Sources Example Actives
Tier 1: Strong Evidence Multiple double-blind, randomized controlled trials (RCTs), independent systematic reviews, Cochrane library consensus. Tretinoin, Salicylic Acid (2%), Niacinamide (2-5%), Broad Spectrum Zinc Oxide / UV Filters, Glycerin.
Tier 2: Moderate Evidence Published peer-reviewed in-vivo trials, consistent split-face clinical studies, established dermatological use. Azelaic Acid (10%), Alpha Arbutin (2%), Tranexamic Acid (3%), Centella Asiatica, Ceramides (1:3:1 ratio).
Tier 3: Limited / Emerging In-vitro (petri-dish) data, preliminary pilot studies, supplier-sponsored ingredient dossiers without independent validation. Copper Peptides for skin tightening, Bakuchiol (as direct Retinol replacement), Plant Stem Cells.
Tier 4: Marketing Claim Unsubstantiated buzzwords, self-reported consumer perception questionnaires ("92% of 25 people felt glowing"), detox claims. "Anti-pollution shield", "instant pore eraser", "magnetic detox minerals".

4. What Makes Indian Skin & Climate Unique

Most international skincare recommendations are formulated for temperate, low-humidity climates. Applying a heavy occlusive balm designed for cold dry climates in Mumbai or Chennai will often result in clogged pores and prickly heat rashes. We explicitly audit for:

  • Fitzpatrick Skin Types III to VI: Higher baseline epidermal melanin makes South Asian skin prone to Post-Inflammatory Hyperpigmentation (PIH) whenever a harsh active causes barrier irritation. We favor anti-inflammatory buffers.
  • White Cast Detection: Sunscreens must absorb transparently on deeper Indian skin tones without leaving an ashen, blue, or purple halo.
  • Sweat and Sebum Emulsification: In tropical weather, heavy petrolatum or thick plant waxes melt into pores. We prioritize fluid gels, aqua-gels, and dry-touch silicones for day routines.
  • High UV Index & Pollution Synergy: Indian cities frequently experience UV index levels of 9–11 coupled with high PM2.5 concentrations, making antioxidant photoprotection critical.

5. Commercial Integrity & Affiliate Transparency

OraeSkin earns revenue through affiliate commissions when readers purchase products via our Amazon India referral links. Here is how we safeguard editorial objectivity:

  • Rankings Are Not For Sale: No cosmetic brand can purchase a placement on our top lists, manipulate an OraeSkin Score, or sponsor a #1 verdict.
  • Honest Cons & Disadvantages: Every review highlights specific reasons to skip or avoid the product. If a product pills, irritates, or has high fragrance, we state it plainly.
  • Zero Price Inflation: You pay the exact same price on Amazon India whether you click our link or visit directly.
  • Equal Evaluation: Low-cost Indian pharmacy formulations (like Cipla Saslic, Minimalist, Sebogel) are evaluated against identical clinical benchmarks as imported prestige brands.
