## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to repair a damaged skin barrier?
With complete cessation of active acids and dedicated ceramide replenishment, superficial barriers heal within 2 to 4 weeks, while deep chronic barrier dysfunction takes 6 to 8 weeks.

### Why does even water sting my face?
When your stratum corneum lacks barrier lipids, microscopic nerve endings become exposed to ambient air and water minerals, sending sharp pain signals to the brain.
