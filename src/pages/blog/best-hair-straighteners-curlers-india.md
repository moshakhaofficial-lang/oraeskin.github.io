Straighteners and curlers touch your hair directly at 150–230°C, so they can damage it more than any other hair tool. The most important feature isn't the brand. It's **being able to choose a lower temperature**.

**Rough temperature guide:**
- **Fine, thin or chemically treated hair:** 150–170°C.
- **Normal hair:** 170–190°C.
- **Very thick or coarse hair:** up to about 200°C.

Going higher rarely helps and causes more breakage. Always use a heat protectant, style **dry** hair only, and pass over each section once or twice rather than holding the tool in place.

## How to Straighten or Curl Safely

1. **Start with completely dry hair.** Wet hair plus a hot plate can badly damage it. Dry first with a [hair dryer](/blog/best-hair-dryers-under-2000-india/) on medium heat.
2. **Use a heat protectant.** [Bblunt Hot Shot](/reviews/bblunt-hot-shot-heat-protection-mist/) (₹374) or [Streax Heat Protectant](/reviews/streax-heat-protectant-spray/) (₹215).
3. **Start at the lowest temperature** and only go up if it isn't working.
4. **Work in small sections** with one or two slow passes. Don't clamp in one place.
5. **Give hair heat-free days**, and trim split ends regularly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What temperature should I straighten my hair at?
Start around 150–170°C for fine or treated hair and 170–190°C for normal hair, and only go higher for very thick hair. Tools with adjustable settings, like the [Havells HS4222](/reviews/havells-hs4222-wide-hair-straightener/), make this easy.

### Straightening brush or flat iron?
A brush gives a softer, natural look and is quicker for frizz. A flat iron gives sleeker, straighter results. If you mainly want to tame frizz, choose the [Philips brush](/reviews/philips-hair-straightener-brush/).

### Which is the best value?
The [Havells HS4101](/reviews/havells-hs4101-hair-straightener/) at ₹920 is cheapest. The [Philips Selfie](/reviews/philips-selfie-hair-straightener/) at about ₹1,060 adds a lower temperature range. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 26 November 2026 and change often.

### Can I straighten my hair every day?
It's best not to. Daily high heat leads to dryness and breakage. If you must, use the lowest setting, a heat protectant, and give your hair regular breaks.
