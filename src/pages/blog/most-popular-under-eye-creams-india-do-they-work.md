Under-eye creams are among Amazon India's most-reviewed skin care products. They are also among the lowest-rated: most of the bestsellers here sit between 3.7 and 3.8 stars. The reason is usually expectations, not bad products.

**Dark circles have different causes:**
- **Pigmented (brown) circles** are common in South Asian skin and often hereditary. Creams can soften them slightly over months but rarely remove them.
- **Hollows and shadows** come from your bone structure and thin skin. No cream changes these.
- **Puffiness** from sleep, salt or allergies can improve briefly with caffeine and cold.
- **Fine lines and crepiness** respond best to retinoids and peptides, used for several months.

**What helps most:** sleep, sunscreen and sunglasses, and treating allergies. After that, a cream with **retinoids, caffeine or peptides** is worth trying. Expect gradual improvement over two to three months, not quick results.

## How to Use an Eye Cream

- **Use a rice-grain amount for both eyes**, tapped gently with your ring finger along the bone, not on the eyelid.
- **Retinol products go on at night only**, and you need sunscreen and sunglasses by day.
- **Keep caffeine gels in the fridge** for extra de-puffing in the morning.
- **Give it 8–12 weeks** before judging.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do under-eye creams have such low ratings?
Most dark circles in Indian skin are hereditary pigment or shadows from bone structure, which creams can't fully fix. Buyers expecting dramatic results are often disappointed. Puffiness and fine lines respond better.

### Which is best for dark circles?
For pigmented circles, retinoid products such as the [Conscious Chemist](/reviews/conscious-chemist-retinol-eye-cream/) cream or Minimalist's retinal cream have the best chance, used with daily sun protection. Expect gradual softening, not removal.

### Which is the best value?
[Bella Vita EyeLift](/reviews/bella-vita-eyelift-cream/) is cheapest per ml at about ₹10, followed by [Himalaya](/reviews/himalaya-under-eye-cream/) at about ₹10.7. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 22 November 2026 and change often.

### Are any of these fragrance-free?
Only the [Conscious Chemist](/reviews/conscious-chemist-retinol-eye-cream/) cream. The skin around the eyes is thin and reacts easily, so fragrance-free is a sensible choice there.
