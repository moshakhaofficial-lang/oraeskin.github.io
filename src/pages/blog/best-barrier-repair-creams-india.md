Your skin barrier is the outer layer that keeps water in and irritants out. It is made of skin cells held together by **lipids: ceramides, cholesterol and fatty acids**.

**Signs it is damaged:** stinging when you apply products, tightness after washing, redness, flaking, and sudden breakouts or sensitivity. Common causes in India are **over-exfoliating (acids, scrubs), strong retinoids, harsh face washes, salon bleach or facials, and hot water**.

**What helps it recover:**
1. **Stop all actives** (acids, retinoids, vitamin C, scrubs) for 1–2 weeks.
2. Use a **gentle, fragrance-free cleanser** and lukewarm water.
3. Apply a **barrier cream** with ceramides, panthenol or petrolatum twice a day.
4. Keep using **sunscreen**, choosing a gentle one.

Mild damage usually settles within 1–2 weeks; more severe irritation can take a month. **See a dermatologist** if skin is raw, weeping, very swollen, or not improving after two weeks. These are the barrier creams we rate most highly.
