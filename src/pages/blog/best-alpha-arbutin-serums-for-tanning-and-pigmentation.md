## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is Alpha Arbutin different from Beta Arbutin?
Alpha Arbutin has significantly higher stability and up to 10 times higher tyrosinase inhibitory potency than Beta Arbutin, delivering faster and more uniform pigment reduction.

### Can I use Alpha Arbutin with Niacinamide?
Yes! Combining Alpha Arbutin (which blocks melanin production) with Niacinamide (which blocks melanin transfer) is one of the most effective dermatologist-recommended regimens for stubborn melasma.
