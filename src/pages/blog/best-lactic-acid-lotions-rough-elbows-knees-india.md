Elbows and knees get rough and dark because of **constant friction, pressure and thicker skin**. Lactic acid is a good fit for them.

**Lactic acid is an AHA** that loosens dead skin. It is also a **humectant** that draws water into the skin, so it exfoliates and moisturises at the same time. It is generally gentler than glycolic acid.

**How to use it:**
1. Apply after your shower to dry skin, **once a day, ideally at night**.
2. Avoid freshly shaved or waxed skin, and any cuts.
3. Wear sunscreen on exposed areas such as arms, as AHAs increase sun sensitivity.

Expect smoother skin within 2–4 weeks. Darkening on elbows and knees fades more slowly, often over a few months. These are the lactic acid and exfoliating body lotions we rate most highly.
