## Frequently Asked Questions

### What causes dark lips?
Common causes are sun exposure, genetics, lip licking, smoking, and reactions to lipsticks or toothpaste. Sudden dark patches, or patches that change shape, should be checked by a doctor rather than treated with brightening products.
