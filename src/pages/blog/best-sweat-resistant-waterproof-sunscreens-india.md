**No sunscreen is truly "waterproof" or "sweatproof".** A sunscreen labelled **water-resistant** has been tested to keep its SPF for **40 or 80 minutes** in water (some brands claim longer). After that, or after towelling off, you need to reapply.

**When to choose a water-resistant sunscreen:**
- Swimming, sports, outdoor workouts and long commutes in the heat.
- Days you will sweat a lot, such as outdoor events or travel.

**Tips:**
1. Apply **15–20 minutes before** going out or into the water.
2. **Reapply every 80–120 minutes** when swimming or sweating, and always after drying with a towel.
3. Keep it out of your eyes. Sweat can carry sunscreen into them and make them sting.
