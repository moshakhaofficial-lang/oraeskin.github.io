## How to Use Sunscreen When You Sweat or Swim

1. **Apply 15–20 minutes before** going out or into the water, about two finger-lengths for the face and neck.
2. **Reapply every 80 minutes while swimming or sweating**, and immediately after towelling off, whatever the label says.
3. **Pat sweat away instead of wiping**, which removes sunscreen faster.
4. **Keep it out of your eyes.** Apply less on the forehead and brow bone, or use a stick there.
5. **Add a cap, sunglasses and shade.** Sunscreen alone isn't enough for long hours outdoors.
6. **Wash it off properly at night.** Water-resistant formulas cling to skin; see [how to remove sunscreen properly](/blog/how-to-remove-sunscreen-properly-double-cleansing/).

### What is the best waterproof sunscreen in India?
No sunscreen is fully waterproof. For swimming and sports, [Dot & Key Lime Rush](/reviews/dotkey-lime-rush-sports-spf50/) has the longest claim here (180 minutes). For sweaty, oily skin, [Re'equil Ultra Matte](/reviews/reequil-ultra-matte-spf50/) stays matte in heat.

### Which sunscreen is best for sweat?

### How often should I reapply a water-resistant sunscreen?
Every 80 minutes while swimming or sweating, and immediately after drying with a towel. On a normal day outdoors, every 2–3 hours.

### Is a water-resistant sunscreen good for daily use?
Yes, if you like the texture, but it can feel heavier and needs a thorough cleanse at night. For indoor days, a lighter everyday sunscreen is more comfortable.
