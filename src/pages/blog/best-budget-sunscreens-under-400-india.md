## How to Choose a Cheap Sunscreen That Actually Works

| Your skin | Choose | Our pick |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Oily or acne-prone | Gel, fragrance-free | [Deconstruct](/reviews/deconstruct-gel-sunscreen-spf50/) |
| Normal or combination | Light lotion | [Minimalist](/reviews/minimalist-spf50/) |
| Dry | Hydrating, dewy | [Aqualogica](/reviews/aqualogica-radiance-spf50/) or [Foxtale](/reviews/foxtale-glow-sunscreen-spf50/) |
| Sensitive | Fragrance-free | [Deconstruct](/reviews/deconstruct-gel-sunscreen-spf50/) or [Minimalist](/reviews/minimalist-spf50/) |
| Worried about white cast | Tinted | [Lakmé Tinted](/reviews/lakme-tinted-sunscreen-spf50/) |

**What to look for on the label:**
1. **SPF 30–50** for UVB (burning). SPF 50 gives a margin if you under-apply.
2. **PA+++ or PA++++** for UVA, which causes tanning, dark spots and ageing. PA++++ is better.
3. **Modern filters** such as Uvinul A Plus, Tinosorb S or M, or bemotrizinol. They're more stable in strong sun.
4. **No fragrance** if your skin is sensitive.

**How much to use:** about two finger-lengths for the face and neck. Reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors, and after sweating or towelling. For workouts and sweaty days, see our [sweat-resistant sunscreens guide](/blog/best-sweat-resistant-waterproof-sunscreens-india/).
