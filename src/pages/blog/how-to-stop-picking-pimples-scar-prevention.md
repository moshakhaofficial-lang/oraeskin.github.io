Picking pushes bacteria deeper, damages the surrounding skin and greatly increases the risk of dark marks and pitted scars. The most effective way to stop is to **make picking physically harder** and **treat the spot so it heals faster**.

Cover spots with hydrocolloid patches, especially at night and during screen time. Keep a spot treatment handy so you "do something" other than squeeze. Keep nails short. If you pick compulsively or notice scarring, a dermatologist can help. These are the products on Amazon India we rate most highly.
