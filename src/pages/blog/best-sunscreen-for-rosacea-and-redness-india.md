## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does heat make rosacea worse in India?
Heat stimulates the sympathetic nervous system, dilating superficial facial capillaries and flooding tissues with blood, causing persistent redness and stinging.

### Can chemical sunscreens trigger rosacea?
Old-school chemical filters (like Oxybenzone) convert UV light into heat, which can exacerbate flushing. Modern hybrid sunscreens formulated with ceramides are far gentler.
