**Sulfur** has been used on acne for a long time. It is mildly antibacterial and helps dry out and peel the surface of a pimple. Drying lotions combine it with **calamine and zinc oxide** (soothing, oil-absorbing powders) and often **salicylic acid**, in an alcohol base that evaporates fast.

**They work best on pimples that already have a white head.** They do little for deep, painful bumps under the skin.

**How to use a two-layer drying lotion:**
1. **Don't shake the bottle.** Dip a clean cotton bud into the pink sediment at the bottom.
2. Dab it only on the spot, at night, after your moisturiser.
3. Let it dry and wash it off in the morning.

The alcohol base can dry the skin around the spot, so keep it on the pimple. These are the drying treatments we rate most highly.
