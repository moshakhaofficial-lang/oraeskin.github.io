Five moisturizers sit near the top of Amazon India's face care bestseller list, with more than 150,000 ratings between them. They are very different products, though. Choosing the wrong one for your skin type is the most common reason a moisturizer "breaks you out" or "doesn't work".

**The quick rule:**
- **Oily or combination skin, humid weather:** a light, oil-free gel.
- **Dry or flaky skin, winter, AC offices:** a cream with ceramides, butters or oils.
- **Sensitive or easily irritated skin:** a fragrance-free formula.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is NIVEA Soft good for the face?
For normal skin that isn't acne-prone or sensitive, yes. It contains denatured alcohol, lanolin alcohol and perfume, so people with breakouts or reactive skin are better off with a dedicated face moisturizer such as [Re'equil](/reviews/reequil-ceramide-ha-moisturiser/) or [Pond's Super Light Gel](/reviews/ponds-super-light-gel/).

### Which is the best value?
[NIVEA Soft](/reviews/nivea-soft-cream/) is cheapest at about ₹1.1 per ml, followed by [Pond's Super Light Gel](/reviews/ponds-super-light-gel/) at about ₹1.5 per gram. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 19 November 2026 and change often.

### Are any of these fragrance-free?
Only [Cetaphil Moisturising Cream](/reviews/cetaphil-moisturising-cream/). The other four list added fragrance.

### Is Neutrogena Hydro Boost worth the price?
It feels great, but it is mostly silicones, water and glycerin. Pond's Super Light Gel gives a similarly light feel for about a tenth of the price per gram. Buy Hydro Boost if you specifically love its finish under makeup.
