## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does Isotretinoin cause such severe lip peeling?
Isotretinoin shrinks sebaceous glands by up to 90% and accelerates epidermal shedding. Lips have no oil glands, so they completely lose their protective lipid shield.

### Will lip peeling stop once I finish my Accutane course?
Yes! Natural epidermal turnover and lipid production return to normal within 2 to 4 weeks after discontinuing oral isotretinoin.
