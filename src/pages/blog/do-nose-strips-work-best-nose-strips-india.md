Peeling off a nose strip and seeing the gunk on it is satisfying, which is why strips sell so well on Amazon India. But your blackheads are back within days. Here's why.

**What blackheads are:** a plug of oil and dead skin in a pore. The top turns dark when it meets air. It isn't dirt.

**What strips do:** the adhesive grips the top of the plug and pulls part of it out. Your nose looks smoother for a few days, but the pore keeps making oil and refills.

**What works long term:** **salicylic acid** (BHA), which dissolves oil inside pores, used in a daily cleanser or a few times a week as a leave-on product. Strips are fine as an occasional extra.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do nose strips make pores bigger?
No, but using them often can irritate and stretch the skin and cause redness. Pore size is mostly genetic, and clear pores look smaller.

### What are the "white strands" on my nose?
Often they are sebaceous filaments: normal oil-filled pores, not blackheads. They refill naturally and don't need removing. Salicylic acid can make them less visible.

### Which nose strip is the best value?

### How often can I use nose strips?
Once a week at most. More often can irritate skin, especially if you also use exfoliating acids or retinoids.
