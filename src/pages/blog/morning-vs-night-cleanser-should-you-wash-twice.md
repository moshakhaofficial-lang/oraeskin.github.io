**At night, always cleanse.** You need to remove sunscreen, pollution, sweat and oil from the day. If you wear water-resistant sunscreen or makeup, consider double cleansing.

**In the morning,** it depends on your skin. Oily or acne-prone skin usually benefits from a gentle wash. Dry or sensitive skin can often just rinse with water, or use a very mild cleanser, to avoid stripping.

Choose your cleanser by skin type rather than time of day. These are the cleansers on Amazon India we rate most highly for each.
