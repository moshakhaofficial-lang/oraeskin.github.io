## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I really need sunscreen inside an office?
Yes! UVA rays penetrate standard office window glass, and close-proximity blue light from monitors stimulates melanogenesis in South Asian skin.

### Why does my face get oily by afternoon in AC?
AC dries out your skin surface. Dehydrated skin signals sebaceous glands to overproduce sebum as an emergency protective barrier, causing afternoon shine.
