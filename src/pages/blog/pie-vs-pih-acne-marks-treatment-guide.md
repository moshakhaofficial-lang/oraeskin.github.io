## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which takes longer to heal, PIE or PIH?
PIE (red marks) typically fades in 4 to 8 weeks with barrier care. PIH (deep brown spots) can take 3 to 6 months because melanin must be shed through complete cellular turnover.

### Can you have both PIE and PIH together?
Yes, on South Asian skin tones, an acne lesion frequently begins as vascular redness (PIE) that stimulates melanocytes to deposit dark pigment (PIH) over subsequent weeks.
