## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a helmet visor protect me from UV rays?
Standard clear helmet visors block wind and insects, but they offer minimal UV-A filtration unless explicitly rated UV400. You still need broad-spectrum SPF underneath.

### Why does my face feel so dirty after riding?
Exhaust fumes contain microscopic soot and heavy metals that bond with surface sebum. Matte silicone sunscreens prevent this adhesion.
