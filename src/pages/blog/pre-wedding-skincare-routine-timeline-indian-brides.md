## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should a bride get her last facial before the wedding?
A gentle hydrating facial should be done 7 to 10 days before the wedding, never in the final 72 hours when redness or allergic reactions can occur.

### How can I avoid breakouts from wedding stress?
Keep a bottle of gentle salicylic spot treatment handy, sleep 7+ hours, and avoid introducing untested high-strength active serums in the final month.
