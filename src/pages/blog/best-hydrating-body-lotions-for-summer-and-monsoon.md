## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does body lotion make me sweat in summer?
Heavy occlusives (like high-percentage mineral oil or thick petrolatum) form a waterproof barrier that traps perspiration under the skin. Opt for lightweight lotions with shea butter, panthenol, and glycerin.

### How often should I moisturize my body in the monsoon?
Once daily right after your shower is ideal to replenish lipids stripped by soaps and hard tap water.
