## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can vitamin C fade dark circles?
It can help brown, pigment-type dark circles look a little lighter over 8–12 weeks, especially with daily sunscreen. It does little for bluish circles caused by visible veins or for shadows from hollowness, which are mostly structural.
