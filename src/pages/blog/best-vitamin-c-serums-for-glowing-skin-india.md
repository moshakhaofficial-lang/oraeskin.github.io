## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did my Vitamin C serum turn dark orange?
L-Ascorbic acid oxidizes into Dehydroascorbic acid when exposed to air and light, losing its antioxidant efficacy. Ethyl Ascorbic Acid serums (like Plum and Minimalist) are significantly more stable in warm Indian climates.

### Can Vitamin C cause acne breakouts?
Pure Vitamin C at low pH can irritate sensitive pores. If prone to breakouts, opt for Ethyl Ascorbic Acid buffered with Centella like Minimalist 10% Vitamin C.
