Expecting mothers face hormonal acne and chloasma (pregnancy mask) while having to avoid retinoids and high-dose salicylic acid. Here are options generally considered safe in pregnancy. Always check with your doctor before starting any new active.

Pregnancy triggers immense hormonal surges—surging progesterone and estrogen stimulate oil glands to produce sudden cystic acne, while melanocyte-stimulating hormone (MSH) induces dark facial patches known as chloasma or the "pregnancy mask". However, gold-standard dermatological actives like oral/topical retinoids (Tretinoin, Retinol), high-percentage Salicylic acid (>2%), and Hydroquinone are strictly contraindicated due to fetal development risks. Expecting mothers require clinically verified, pregnancy-safe alternatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which skincare ingredients are strictly forbidden during pregnancy?
Avoid topical retinoids (Tretinoin, Retinol, Retinaldehyde), Hydroquinone, high-dose Salicylic Acid (>2%), and chemical sunscreens containing Oxybenzone.

### Is Niacinamide safe during breastfeeding?
Yes. Topical Niacinamide is a water-soluble form of Vitamin B3 that is naturally present in healthy breast milk and does not cross systemic thresholds in harmful quantities.
