Hydrogel eye patches are a **quick fix before an event or makeup**. They hydrate the under-eye area and their cooling effect can reduce puffiness for a few hours, so concealer sits more smoothly.

**What they cannot do:** they will not remove dark circles caused by genetics, pigmentation or hollowness. Results are **temporary**.

**How to use them:**
1. Apply to clean skin, **chilled in the fridge** for extra de-puffing.
2. Leave on for **15–20 minutes**, not overnight unless the brand says so.
3. Pat in the leftover serum, then apply eye cream and makeup.
4. Use the spatula from the jar, not your fingers, to keep the jar clean.

For longer-term dark circle care, an eye cream with caffeine, retinoids or niacinamide, plus sleep and sunscreen, does more. These are the eye patches we rate most highly.
