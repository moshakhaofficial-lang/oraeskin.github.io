**Ice** suits **red, swollen, painful pimples**, especially deep ones without a head. Wrap ice in a clean cloth and hold it for 5–10 minutes a few times a day to reduce swelling and pain.

A **warm compress** suits **pimples that are forming a head**. It can help them come to the surface and drain naturally. Use a clean, warm (not hot) cloth for 10 minutes, 2–3 times a day.

Never put ice directly on skin, and never squeeze. Once a head forms, a hydrocolloid patch is the cleaner way to let it drain. These are the follow-up products on Amazon India we rate most highly.
