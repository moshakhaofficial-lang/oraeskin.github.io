## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do alcohol aftershaves burn so much?
Alcohol causes intense vasoconstriction and triggers TRPV1 heat/pain receptors on newly abraded skin. It provides no moisturizing benefit and worsens razor bumps.

Prevent ingrowns using Sebogel, and apply Minimalist SPF 50 every morning; UV rays darken post-inflammatory post-shave marks.
