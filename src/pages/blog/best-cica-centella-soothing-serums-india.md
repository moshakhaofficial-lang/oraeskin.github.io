### What makes Centella Asiatica (Cica) so effective?
Centella stimulates collagen type I synthesis, accelerates micro-wound healing, and dramatically decreases inflammatory cytokines (IL-1beta, TNF-alpha) in human dermal fibroblasts.

### Can Cica help with active acne redness?
Yes! Cica's anti-inflammatory properties significantly reduce the red halo (erythema) surrounding active acne papules.
