A tan on your arms is your skin's response to sun exposure. **It fades on its own over weeks to months once you protect the skin**, so the single most effective step is daily sunscreen on exposed areas.

**Brightening lotions can help it fade faster and look more even:**
- **Niacinamide** reduces the transfer of pigment to skin cells and has good evidence.
- **Vitamin C** is an antioxidant that also helps with uneven tone.
- **AHAs** such as lactic or glycolic acid remove dull surface skin.
- **Glutathione** is heavily marketed, but evidence for it working when **applied to the skin** is limited.

**Realistic results:** a more even, brighter look over **6–12 weeks**, not a change in your natural skin colour. Pair any of these with a body sunscreen. These are the brightening lotions we rate most highly.
