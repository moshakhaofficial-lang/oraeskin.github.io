Body oils give a **soft, glowy finish** and seal in moisture. The trick to keeping them from feeling greasy is **when** you apply them.

**How to use body oil:**
1. Apply to **damp skin straight after the shower**. Pat yourself half-dry, then massage in a small amount.
2. Use a few drops per limb. Too much is what makes oils sticky.
3. For very dry skin, layer it over a lotion instead.

**Which oil for which skin:**
- **Dry skin:** almost any body oil helps. Mineral oil and plant butters seal moisture especially well.
- **Acne-prone back or chest:** avoid heavy coconut oil and isopropyl myristate on those areas. A lighter oil is a safer choice.
- **Sensitive skin:** many body oils are heavily fragranced, so patch-test first.
