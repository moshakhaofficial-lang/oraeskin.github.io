Horizontal neck lines come from **repeated bending (looking down at phones), sun exposure and natural ageing**. Neck skin is thinner than facial skin, so it shows lines earlier.

**What has the best evidence:**
- **Daily sunscreen on the neck.** Most people stop at the jawline.
- **Retinoids** help with fine lines and texture over months. Neck skin is sensitive, so start 2–3 nights a week.
- **Peptides and moisturisers** make lines look softer, especially when skin is dry.
- **Posture:** holding your phone at eye level reduces constant creasing.

**Be realistic:** no cream will "lift" or "firm" sagging skin the way procedures can. Products marketed as neck creams are usually rich moisturisers. Your face retinoid and sunscreen work just as well on the neck. These are the products we rate most highly.

1. **Cleanse Thoroughly**: Always start with a gentle, non-stripping cleanser like [CeraVe Foaming Cleanser](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B07C5SKVL7?tag=oraeskin-21).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long before I see noticeable results?
Hydrating and soothing products feel better within days. Acne actives usually need 4–8 weeks, and pigment or anti-ageing actives 8–12 weeks of consistent use alongside daily sunscreen.

### Will this cause breakouts in humid weather?
Most picks above are light formulas, but any product can clog pores for some people. Introduce one new product at a time and give it a week before adding the next, especially if your skin is acne-prone.
