## Frequently Asked Questions

### How should a beginner start retinol?
Use a pea-sized amount on dry skin 2 nights a week for the first two weeks, then 3–4 nights if your skin is comfortable. Moisturise afterwards, avoid using strong acids on the same night, and wear sunscreen every morning. Skip retinoids if you are pregnant or trying to conceive.
