If your skin feels tight and itchy after a bath, your soap may be the problem, not just the weather. That is especially likely in winter or if you have hard water.

**Not all "soaps" are the same:**
- **True soap** is made by reacting oils with an alkali. It is alkaline (pH around 9–10), while skin is slightly acidic (around 5). Soap removes oil well but can leave dry skin tight.
- **Syndet bars and body washes** use milder synthetic cleansers such as sodium cocoyl isethionate. They are closer to skin's pH and much gentler on dry skin.

**For dry skin:** choose a syndet bar or a mild body wash, keep showers short and warm, and apply body lotion within three minutes of drying off.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is soap bad for dry skin?
Traditional soap is alkaline and can strip oils, which makes dry skin tighter and itchier. Syndet bars such as the [Dove Serum Bar](/reviews/dove-serum-bar/) and mild body washes are gentler choices.

### Bar or body wash: which is better?
For dry skin, the type of cleanser matters more than the format. A syndet bar and a mild body wash are both fine. Body washes are more hygienic for families, because each person doesn't share the same bar.

### Which is the best value?
[Pears Pure & Gentle Body Wash](/reviews/pears-pure-gentle-body-wash/) is cheapest per ml at about ₹0.38. Among bars, [Mysore Sandal](/reviews/mysore-sandal-soap/) is cheapest per gram, but it suits dry skin least. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 22 November 2026 and change often.
