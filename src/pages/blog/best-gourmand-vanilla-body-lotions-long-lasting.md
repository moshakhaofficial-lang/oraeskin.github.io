"Gourmand" lotions smell sweet and edible, with notes like **vanilla, caramel and cocoa**. They are mainly about scent, so the best ones are **also good, simple moisturisers**.

**How to make the scent last longer:**
1. Apply to damp skin right after showering.
2. **Layer**: use a vanilla body oil or body wash with your lotion.
3. Reapply a little on pulse points (wrists, inner elbows) later in the day.

**Worth knowing:** fragrance is the main ingredient you are paying for, and it is one of the most common causes of skin irritation. Skip scented lotions on **broken, eczema-prone or freshly shaved skin**, and patch-test if your skin is sensitive. These are the gourmand lotions we rate most highly.
