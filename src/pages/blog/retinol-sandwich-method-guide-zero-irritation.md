The **retinol sandwich** layers moisturiser, then retinoid, then moisturiser again. The first layer slightly slows how fast the retinoid penetrates, and the top layer seals in hydration. Together they reduce dryness, flaking and stinging, especially in your first months.

**How to do it:** cleanse and pat skin completely dry, apply a thin layer of moisturiser, wait a minute, apply a pea-sized amount of retinoid, then finish with another layer of moisturiser. Start 2–3 nights a week, and wear sunscreen every morning.

The trade-off is that buffering can slightly reduce strength, which is usually worth it for sensitive skin. These are the products on Amazon India we rate most highly for each layer.
