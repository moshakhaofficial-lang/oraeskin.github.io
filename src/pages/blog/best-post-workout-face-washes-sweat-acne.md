## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does sweat alone cause acne?
No, fresh sweat is mostly water and minerals. Acne occurs when sweat mixes with surface sebum, dead skin cells, and gym bacteria inside follicular funnels.

### Can I just splash water on my face after gym?
Splashing plain water removes surface salts, but it cannot dissolve lipophilic sebum and gym grime. A gentle cleanser is necessary.
