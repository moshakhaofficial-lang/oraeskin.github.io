An effective anti-ageing routine has just four essentials: **gentle cleansing, an antioxidant in the morning, daily sunscreen, and a retinoid at night**. Sunscreen does the most work — UV causes most visible ageing and pigmentation in Indian skin.

**Morning:** cleanse, apply vitamin C, moisturise, then sunscreen. **Night:** cleanse, apply a retinoid 2–5 nights a week, then moisturise. Add an eye cream if lines or dark circles bother you. These are the products on Amazon India we rate most highly for each step.
