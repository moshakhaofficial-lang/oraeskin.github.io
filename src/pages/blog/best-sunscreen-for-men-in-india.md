## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does sunscreen cause breakouts for men?
No, provided you choose an oil-free, non-comedogenic formulation. Breakouts typically occur when heavy creams combine with trapped sweat, which gel and hybrid sunscreens prevent.

### Can I apply sunscreen directly over my beard?
Yes, but use fluid formulations like Minimalist SPF 50 or Derma Co Aqua Gel. Massage thoroughly through hair shafts down to the skin barrier underneath.
