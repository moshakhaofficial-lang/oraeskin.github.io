## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between Retinol and Granactive Retinoid?
Traditional Retinol requires a two-step metabolic conversion (Retinol -> Retinal -> Retinoic Acid), releasing irritating byproducts that cause peeling. Granactive Retinoid (HPR) binds directly to cellular receptors, providing similar anti-aging benefits with minimal irritation.

### How long before I see anti-aging results from retinoids?
Softening of skin texture and improved hydration are visible within 4 to 6 weeks. Significant reduction in fine lines and firmer collagen density typically require 12 to 16 weeks of consistent use.
