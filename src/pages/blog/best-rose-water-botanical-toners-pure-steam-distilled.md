Real rose water is a **hydrosol**: the water left over when rose petals are steam-distilled. Many cheaper "rose waters" are instead **water plus rose oil and added fragrance**. Check the ingredient list: a pure one lists only rose flower water (*Rosa damascena* flower water).

**What rose water can do:** it is a pleasant, mildly soothing and hydrating mist, and works well for mixing face packs. It won't treat acne, pigmentation or large pores, whatever the label says.

Pure rose water usually has no preservative, so keep it cool and use it within a few months. If you have sensitive skin, avoid fragranced versions. These are the rose waters on Amazon India we rate most highly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long before I see noticeable results?
Hydrating and soothing products feel better within days. Acne actives usually need 4–8 weeks, and pigment or anti-ageing actives 8–12 weeks of consistent use alongside daily sunscreen.

### Will this cause breakouts in humid weather?
Most picks above are light formulas, but any product can clog pores for some people. Introduce one new product at a time and give it a week before adding the next, especially if your skin is acne-prone.
