## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should I start using an anti-aging eye cream?
Dermatologists recommend introducing preventative hydrating eye treatments in your mid-20s, before dynamic expression lines become permanently etched static wrinkles.

### Can peptides replace botox for crow's feet?
Peptides cannot freeze dynamic muscle contractions like neurotoxin injections, but they significantly boost dermal collagen density and smooth fine surface crepey lines over 8-12 weeks.
