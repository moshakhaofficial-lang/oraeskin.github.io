## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How much sunscreen should I apply on Indian skin?
Use the **two-finger rule**: apply two full lines of sunscreen along the length of your index and middle finger. This ensures you reach the 2mg/cm² coverage required to achieve the full SPF 50 protection stated on the label.

### Does SPF 50 prevent tanning?
Yes, high PA ratings (PA++++ indicates high UVA defense) prevent melanin synthesis triggered by UVA rays, which is the primary cause of tanning and dark spots.

### Do I need to double cleanse to remove sunscreen?
If you wear water-resistant sunscreen, using a micellar water or gentle oil-based wash first ensures all UV filters are removed without clogging pores.
