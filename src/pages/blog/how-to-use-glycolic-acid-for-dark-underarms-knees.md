Darker underarms, knees and elbows are very common in Indian skin. Usual causes are **friction, shaving or waxing irritation, thicker skin build-up and post-inflammatory pigmentation**. Glycolic acid helps by loosening built-up dead skin, which can make these areas look smoother and more even over time.

**How to do it safely:**
1. Use it at night on **clean, completely dry skin**, never right after shaving or waxing (wait 48 hours).
2. Swipe a cotton pad with glycolic toner over the area **2–3 times a week** to start.
3. Let it dry. Skip deodorant that night if it stings.
4. Moisturise elbows and knees afterwards.
5. Wear sunscreen on exposed areas like knees and elbows during the day.

**Expect gradual results over 6–12 weeks.** Stop if you get burning, rash or more darkening. If the darkening is velvety, thick or came on suddenly, see a doctor, as it can be linked to insulin resistance (acanthosis nigricans). These are the products we rate most highly for this.
