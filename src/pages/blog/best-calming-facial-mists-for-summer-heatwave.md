## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can facial mists actually dehydrate skin if not sealed?
Yes, pure water mists will evaporate into dry air and pull skin moisture with them. However, mists formulated with humectants (like glycerin and mineral waters) bind moisture to the stratum corneum.

### Can I mist over makeup and sunscreen?
Yes, fine mist nozzles deposit microscopic droplets that set makeup and refresh the skin without dissolving your underlying sunscreen film.
