## Editorial Routine Tips for Indian Skin

3. **Always Reapply**: Reapply your sunscreen every 80 minutes of continuous swimming or immediately after towel drying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do waterproof sunscreens really stay on in water?
Under FDA and ISO standards, 'water-resistant' sunscreens maintain their labeled SPF for 40 to 80 minutes of active water immersion. No sunscreen is truly waterproof forever.

### How do I remove waterproof sunscreen after swimming?
Water-resistant silicone sunscreens require an oil-based cleanser or a gentle micellar wash followed by a foaming wash to fully dissolve.
