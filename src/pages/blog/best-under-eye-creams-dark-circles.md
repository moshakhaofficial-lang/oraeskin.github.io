## Which Type of Dark Circle Do You Have?

Look in a mirror in daylight and gently stretch the skin under your eye.

- **Brown or grey colour that stays when you stretch the skin:** pigment, often hereditary in South Asian skin and worse with sun and rubbing. **Helps:** retinoids, daily sunscreen, sunglasses, not rubbing. Cooling gels alone won't.
- **Bluish or purple tint, worse when you're tired:** thin skin showing blood vessels. **Helps:** retinoids (which thicken skin over months), caffeine, sleep. Don't expect fast results.
- **Puffy bags, worst in the morning:** fluid build-up from sleep, salt or allergies. **Helps:** caffeine, cold (a chilled roller or gel patches), less salt at night. Avoid heavy, oily creams.
- **Shadow or hollow that changes with lighting:** bone structure and volume loss. **Helps:** concealer; only in-clinic fillers change it. No cream will.
- **Fine lines and crêpey texture:** ageing, dryness and sun. **Helps:** retinoids, peptides, moisturiser. Caffeine alone won't.

Most people have a mix. A retinoid cream at night plus sunscreen by day covers the most common combination in Indian skin, which is pigment plus thin skin.

## How to Choose an Under-Eye Cream

1. **Match the active to your dark circle** using the table above: retinoids for pigment, thin skin and lines; caffeine for puffiness.
2. **Choose fragrance-free.** Under-eye skin is thin and reacts easily. Five of the seven picks here are unscented.
3. **Check that the strength is stated.** "Retinal 0.1%" or "5% caffeine" tells you more than "with retinol".
4. **Start a retinoid slowly:** every other night for two weeks, at night only, with sunscreen by day.
5. **Give it 8–12 weeks.** Pigment and thin skin change slowly; puffiness responds within minutes but only lasts hours.

**What matters as much as the cream:** sunscreen tapped up to the eye bone every morning, sunglasses outdoors, not rubbing your eyes, enough sleep, and treating allergies. For hereditary circles, read [how to treat hereditary dark circles in South Asian skin](/blog/how-to-treat-hereditary-dark-circles-south-asian-skin/).

**For instant de-puffing**, cold beats any cream: a chilled roller, [cooling gel eye patches](/blog/under-eye-patches-masks-emergency-event-prep/) or an ice roller (compared in our [facial steamer vs ice roller guide](/blog/facial-steamer-vs-ice-roller-india/)).

## How to Apply Eye Cream

1. Use a **rice-grain amount for both eyes**.
2. **Tap it with your ring finger** along the eye bone, from the inner corner outwards. Don't rub.
3. Keep retinoids **a few millimetres below the lash line** and off the eyelid.
4. Apply sunscreen around the eyes every morning, especially if you use a retinoid.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best under-eye cream for dark circles in India?
For most people, the [Minimalist Vitamin K + Retinal 0.1%](/reviews/minimalist-caffeine-eye-serum/) cream, because retinal works on pigment, thin skin and fine lines, and it adds caffeine for puffiness. If your main issue is morning puffiness, [The Ordinary Caffeine Solution](/reviews/theordinary-caffeine-egcg/) is the better pick.

### Can an eye cream remove dark circles completely?
Usually not. Hereditary pigment and shadows from bone structure don't disappear with any cream. A retinoid plus daily sunscreen can lighten pigment and thin-skin circles over two to three months. Puffiness improves quickly but returns.

### Which eye cream is best for puffy eyes?
Caffeine products: [The Ordinary Caffeine Solution 5% + EGCG](/reviews/theordinary-caffeine-egcg/), or [The Derma Co caffeine serum](/reviews/dermaco-5-caffeine-serum/) if you like a cold roller. Keeping them in the fridge helps.

### Is there a good niacinamide eye cream?
Of our picks, [Beauty of Joseon Revive Eye Serum](/reviews/boj-revive-eye-serum/) contains niacinamide alongside retinal. Niacinamide can help even out tone, but a retinoid usually does more for dark circles.

### Are these eye creams suitable for women and men?
Yes. Under-eye skin works the same way regardless of gender, so choose by your type of dark circle, not by the packaging.

### Which is the best value?
[Conscious Chemist](/reviews/conscious-chemist-retinol-eye-cream/) at ₹279 is the cheapest retinoid, and [mCaffeine](/reviews/mcaffeine-coffee-eye-cream/) at ₹261 is the cheapest caffeine option. [The Ordinary](/reviews/theordinary-caffeine-egcg/) costs more upfront but lasts longest at 30ml. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 27 November 2026 and change often.

### Can I use an under-eye cream with a retinoid while pregnant?
No. Avoid retinal and retinol during pregnancy and breastfeeding. A caffeine or peptide product is a safer choice; check with your doctor.
