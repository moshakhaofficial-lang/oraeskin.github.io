Cracked, peeling lips usually come from **dry weather, licking your lips, mouth breathing or a reaction to a lip product**, such as one with fragrance, menthol or cinnamon flavour.

What helps is a **simple, fragrance-free balm** with soothing ingredients (panthenol, bisabolol), occlusives (waxes, butters, oils) and, for daytime, **SPF**.

**Sore, cracked corners of the mouth (angular cheilitis)** are a little different. They are often infected with yeast or bacteria, or linked to low iron or B vitamins, ill-fitting dentures or drooling during sleep. A barrier balm helps comfort, but **if the corners do not heal within two weeks, see a doctor**, as you may need an antifungal or antibacterial cream.

**Tips:**
- Stop licking your lips, as saliva dries them more.
- Avoid flavoured or minty balms while healing.
- Apply a thick layer at night.

These are the balms we rate most highly.
