Lip balms are everyday products, and four of the most-bought on Amazon India have more than 85,000 ratings between them. They do slightly different jobs, though.

**What dry lips need:**
- **An occlusive** such as petroleum jelly, beeswax or butters, to seal moisture in. Lips have almost no oil glands of their own.
- **SPF during the day.** Sun exposure is one of the main causes of darkening lips in India.
- **No licking.** Saliva evaporates and leaves lips drier.

**What to avoid on cracked lips:** strong flavours, menthol, camphor and heavy fragrance can sting and irritate.

## A Simple Lip Routine

- **Morning:** an SPF lip balm, reapplied after eating or drinking.
- **Night:** a thick occlusive balm such as [Vaseline Lip Therapy](/reviews/vaseline-lip-therapy-rosy/), or an overnight [lip sleeping mask](/blog/best-lip-sleeping-masks-chapped-peeling-lips/).
- **Peeling lips:** don't pick or scrub hard. Let a thick balm soften the flakes overnight.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which lip balm is best for very dry, cracked lips?
A petroleum-based balm such as [Vaseline Lip Therapy](/reviews/vaseline-lip-therapy-rosy/), applied thickly at night. If cracks at the corners of your mouth don't heal in two weeks, see a doctor; it can be a minor infection.

### Do I really need SPF on my lips?
In India, yes, for daytime. Sun exposure contributes to darker lips. [Lakmé Lip Love](/reviews/lakme-lip-love-care-stick/) gives basic SPF 15, and SPF 30–50 balms protect better.

### Which is the best value?
[Lakmé Lip Love](/reviews/lakme-lip-love-care-stick/) is cheapest at about ₹24 per gram and is also the only one here with SPF. [NIVEA Original Care](/reviews/nivea-original-care-lip-balm/) is next at about ₹26 per gram. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 22 November 2026 and change often.

### Is Laneige worth the price?
It feels and looks lovely, but its moisturising ingredients are similar to cheaper balms. Buy it for the glossy finish, not because it heals lips better.
