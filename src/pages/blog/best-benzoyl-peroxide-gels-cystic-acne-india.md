**Benzoyl peroxide** is one of the best-studied acne treatments. It kills *C. acnes*, the bacteria involved in red, inflamed pimples, and bacteria do not become resistant to it the way they can to antibiotic creams.

Studies comparing strengths have found **2.5% works about as well as 5% or 10% for most people, with less dryness and peeling**. So start low.

**How to start:**
1. Apply a thin layer to the whole acne-prone area (not just single spots) on clean, dry skin, **every other night** for the first two weeks.
2. Moisturise afterwards. Some dryness and flaking in weeks 1–3 is normal.
3. Use white pillowcases and towels, as it **bleaches fabric**.
4. Wear sunscreen in the day.

**Deep, painful cysts or nodules** usually need a dermatologist, since they often need prescription treatment to prevent scarring. Several of these gels are sold by pharmacy sellers with the note "as directed by the physician", so check with a doctor if you are unsure, pregnant, or using other acne medicines.
