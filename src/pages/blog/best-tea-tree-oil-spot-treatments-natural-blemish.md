**Tea tree oil** has some research support for mild acne. Small trials found that a 5% tea tree gel reduced pimples compared with a placebo, and one trial found it worked about as well as 5% benzoyl peroxide, **though more slowly**.

So it is a reasonable option for **occasional, mild spots**, but not for moderate or cystic acne.

**Use it safely:**
- **Never put pure tea tree essential oil straight on your face.** Undiluted oil can cause irritation and allergic reactions. Choose a ready-diluted product.
- Patch-test on your jaw for two days first.
- Dab it only on the spot, once or twice a day.
- Stop if you get redness or itching, as allergy to tea tree oil is not rare.
