## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can eye creams completely cure dark circles?
If dark circles are caused by dehydration, venous pooling, or surface hyperpigmentation, yes. However, dark circles caused by deep genetic bone hollows can only be disguised with skincare, not eliminated entirely.

### Will thick eye creams cause milia seeds?
Heavy mineral oils and petrolatum can clog delicate orbital follicles and trigger white milia seeds. Stick to lightweight, non-comedogenic creams like CeraVe Eye Repair.
