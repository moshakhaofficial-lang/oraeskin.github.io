Tranexamic acid (TXA) reduces pigment by calming the signals that tell skin to make more melanin after **UV exposure and inflammation**. Topical TXA at **2–5%** is gentle, rarely irritates, and pairs well with niacinamide, arbutin or azelaic acid.

**Who it suits:** stubborn sun spots, post-acne marks, and people whose skin reacts to vitamin C or acids. For **melasma**, it is often used alongside dermatologist treatment, which may include oral TXA (prescription only).

As with every pigment treatment, give it **8–12 weeks** and use SPF 50 daily. These are the TXA serums on Amazon India we rate most highly.
