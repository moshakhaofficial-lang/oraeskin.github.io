Snail secretion filtrate ("snail mucin") contains **glycoproteins, hyaluronic acid and allantoin**. It works as a hydrating, soothing layer that leaves skin looking dewy, and small studies suggest it may help with healing and acne marks.

**How to use it:** apply an essence after cleansing on slightly damp skin, then follow with moisturiser. Oily skin can often use the essence alone in humid weather.
