**Keratosis pilaris (KP)** causes small, rough bumps, usually on the upper arms, thighs and buttocks. Each bump is a **plug of dead skin (keratin) in a hair follicle**. It is very common, harmless and often runs in families.

**"Strawberry legs"** describes dark dots on the legs. These come from clogged or darkened pores and hair follicles, and are often made worse by shaving.

**What helps:**
- **Urea (10–20%)** softens and loosens the keratin plugs while moisturising.
- **AHAs (lactic or glycolic acid) and salicylic acid** exfoliate the plugs away.
- **Gentle hair removal**: shave with a sharp razor and gel, or consider waxing or laser for strawberry legs.

KP can be controlled but not cured. **Expect improvement after 4–8 weeks of daily use**, and it will come back if you stop. Do not scrub hard, as that makes the bumps redder. These are the lotions we rate most highly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long before I see noticeable results?
Hydrating and soothing products feel better within days. Acne actives usually need 4–8 weeks, and pigment or anti-ageing actives 8–12 weeks of consistent use alongside daily sunscreen.

### Will this cause breakouts in humid weather?
Most picks above are light formulas, but any product can clog pores for some people. Introduce one new product at a time and give it a week before adding the next, especially if your skin is acne-prone.
