Glycolic acid smooths texture and fades dullness, but melanin-rich skin is prone to **post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation**. Over-exfoliating can actually cause dark patches. Use it safely:

1. **Start low and slow:** 2–3 nights a week, on dry skin.
2. **Don't combine** it with retinoids or other acids on the same night.
3. **Moisturise afterwards**, and stop if you get stinging, peeling or redness that lasts.
4. **Wear SPF 50 every morning.** AHAs increase sun sensitivity.
