Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) is the most widespread concern for Indian skin tones. Melanin-dense complexions overproduce pigment in response to acne inflammation, leaving stubborn brown and purple marks that linger for months. Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) prevents the transfer of melanin packets (melanosomes) from melanocytes to surface keratinocytes, while calming sebum output.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does 10% Niacinamide cause skin purging?
No, Niacinamide does not increase cellular turnover like retinoids or AHAs, so it does not cause clinical purging. Temporary irritation is usually due to formulation sensitivity or applying too many drops at once.

### How long does it take for acne marks to fade?
With twice-daily application and diligent morning sun protection, visible reduction in acne mark intensity is typically observed within 4 to 6 weeks.
