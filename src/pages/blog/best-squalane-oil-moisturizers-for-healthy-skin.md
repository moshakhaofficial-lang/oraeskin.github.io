**Squalane** is a stable, lightweight oil made from plant sources such as olives or sugarcane. It is very similar to **squalene**, a natural part of your skin's own oil.

That makes it one of the **least irritating, least greasy oils** you can use. It suits dry, sensitive and even many oily skin types.

**What it does:** squalane is an **emollient and occlusive**. It softens skin and reduces water loss. It does not add hydration by itself, so **pair it with a water-based serum or moisturiser**.

**How to use it:**
- Add 2–3 drops to your moisturiser, or press it in as the last step at night.
- Use it on damp skin after a hydrating serum.
- It also works on dry cuticles, lips and the ends of your hair.
