Old-style toners were astringent and often alcohol-based. **Hydrating toners** do the opposite: they add a first layer of water-binding ingredients such as hyaluronic acid, glycerin and NMFs. **Milky toners** add light emollients and ceramides, so they feel more cushioning.

**How to use them:** pat a few drops into skin straight after cleansing, while it is still slightly damp. For a "glass skin" look, you can layer 2–3 thin coats, then seal with moisturiser.

Pick a **milky toner** if your skin is dry, and a **watery toner** if it is oily. These are the hydrating toners on Amazon India we rate most highly.
