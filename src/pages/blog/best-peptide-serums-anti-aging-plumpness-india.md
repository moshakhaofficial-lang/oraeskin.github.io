Peptides are short chains of amino acids. **Signal peptides** such as Matrixyl 3000 are thought to encourage collagen production, and **copper peptides** support repair. Their main advantage is that they **don't irritate**, so they can be used daily, even on sensitive skin.

**Be realistic:** the evidence for peptides is much smaller than for retinoids. Think of them as a gentle add-on that improves hydration and smoothness, not a replacement for a retinoid and sunscreen.

**Pairing tips:** apply peptides on their own, separate from strong acids. Keep copper peptides away from L-ascorbic acid (vitamin C) in the same routine. These are the peptide serums on Amazon India we rate most highly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long before I see noticeable results?
Hydrating and soothing products feel better within days. Acne actives usually need 4–8 weeks, and pigment or anti-ageing actives 8–12 weeks of consistent use alongside daily sunscreen.

### Will this cause breakouts in humid weather?
Most picks above are light formulas, but any product can clog pores for some people. Introduce one new product at a time and give it a week before adding the next, especially if your skin is acne-prone.
