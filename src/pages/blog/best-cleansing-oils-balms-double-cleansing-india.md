**Double cleansing** means using an oil-based cleanser first to dissolve sunscreen, makeup and sebum, then a gentle face wash to clean the skin. It's most useful at night if you wear water-resistant sunscreen or makeup.

A good balm melts on dry skin, turns milky when you add water, and rinses off without a greasy film. These are the cleansing balms on Amazon India we rate most highly.
