## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does air pollution cause premature skin aging?
Nanoparticles in smog penetrate epidermal barriers and trigger AhR (Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor) signaling, which upregulates MMP enzymes that chew up healthy collagen and elastin.

### Can sunscreen alone protect against air pollution?
Sunscreen blocks UV light, but unless it contains antioxidants, it cannot neutralize the chemical free radicals generated by diesel fumes and smog.
