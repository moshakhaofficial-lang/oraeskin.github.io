Exfoliating pads are cotton pads pre-soaked in an acid toner. They're convenient for travel and quick routines. The trade-off is **cost**: they're usually far more expensive per use than pouring the same type of toner onto your own cotton pad.

**Choose by acid:** PHA pads are gentlest (good for sensitive skin), BHA (salicylic acid) pads suit oily and acne-prone skin, and AHA (glycolic) suits dullness and texture.

**Don't over-exfoliate:** start every other night, swipe gently rather than scrubbing, and stop if skin turns tight, shiny or stings. These are the exfoliating pads, and one budget alternative, on Amazon India we rate most highly.
