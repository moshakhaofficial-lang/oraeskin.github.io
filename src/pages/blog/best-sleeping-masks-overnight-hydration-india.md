Sleeping with air conditioning drops ambient humidity to desert levels (under 30%), causing severe morning tightness. Discover lightweight overnight sleeping packs.

During hot Indian summers and monsoons, sleeping with air conditioning is essential for comfortable rest. However, AC units continuously extract water vapor from the room, dropping indoor relative humidity below 30%. Over 8 hours of sleep, this causes severe Transepidermal Water Loss (TEWL), leaving you with a parched, tight, and dull morning complexion. Overnight gel masks seal in hydration without staining pillowcases.
