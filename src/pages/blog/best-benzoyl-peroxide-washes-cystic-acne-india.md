A benzoyl peroxide wash is a way to get the acne-fighting effect of benzoyl peroxide **with less irritation than a leave-on gel**. This is called short-contact therapy.

It is especially useful for **chest, back and shoulder acne**, where spreading a gel over a large area is awkward.

**How to use it:**
1. Lather onto wet skin and **leave it on for 1–2 minutes** before rinsing. A quick splash-and-rinse does much less.
2. Start once a day, then go up to twice a day if your skin is comfortable.
3. Rinse thoroughly and use **white towels**, as benzoyl peroxide bleaches fabric.
4. Moisturise afterwards.

For the face, **2.5% is usually enough**. The stronger 5% is better kept for the back and chest. Deep, painful cysts need a dermatologist, not just a wash. These are the washes we rate most highly.
