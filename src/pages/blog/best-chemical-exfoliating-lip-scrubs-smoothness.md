Lip skin is thin and has no oil glands, so it dries and flakes easily. **Exfoliating helps remove flakes, but overdoing it makes lips sorer and flakier.**

**Two gentle ways to do it:**
- **Chemical:** a leave-on lip product with a mild acid such as **PHA (gluconolactone)** loosens flakes with no rubbing.
- **Physical:** a **sugar** scrub dissolves as you rub, so it is gentler than scrubs made with crushed walnut shells or apricot kernels.

**How often:** once or twice a week is plenty. Always follow with a balm, and **never exfoliate cracked, bleeding or cold-sore-affected lips**.

Peeling that won't stop, or lips that keep cracking at the corners, may be caused by a lip product allergy, licking your lips, or a vitamin deficiency. In that case see a doctor rather than exfoliating more. These are the products we rate most highly.
