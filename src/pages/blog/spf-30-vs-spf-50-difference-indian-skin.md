SPF measures protection against **UVB** (burning). Applied correctly, **SPF 30 blocks about 97% of UVB and SPF 50 about 98%**. The difference sounds small, but in real life most people apply far less sunscreen than tested, so SPF 50 gives a useful safety margin in India's strong sun.

Just as important for Indian skin is **UVA protection** (the PA rating), because UVA drives tanning and pigmentation. Look for **PA+++ or PA++++**, apply about two finger-lengths for your face and neck, and reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors.
