UV radiation is the leading hidden cause of hyperpigmented, dark lips in India. Shield and restore lip barriers with ceramides and broad-spectrum SPF 30.

Lips lack sebaceous oil glands, sweat glands, and a protective stratum granulosum layer, making them the most vulnerable cutaneous tissue on the human face. In India's high UV climate, unprotected sun exposure causes melanocytes in the vermilion border to deposit dark pigment, leading to two-toned, pigmented lips. Ordinary flavored petroleum balms offer zero UV defense and can even magnify sun damage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can SPF lip balm lighten already darkened lips?
Yes. By completely blocking UV-stimulated melanogenesis, existing melanin naturally sloughs off over 4 to 6 weeks as lip skin cells renew, revealing lighter, healthier lip tone.

### Why do cheap lip balms make my lips feel drier?
Many budget balms contain camphor, menthol, or artificial flavors that create a cooling sensation while drying out the underlying tissue, creating a cycle of addiction.
