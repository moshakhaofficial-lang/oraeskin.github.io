## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my acne is fungal or bacterial?
Fungal acne typically presents as tiny, uniform, itchy bumps clustered on the forehead, chest, or hairline that do not respond to benzoyl peroxide. Bacterial acne features varying sizes of comedones, pustules, and cysts.

### Can I use natural coconut or olive oil with fungal acne?
No! Coconut oil and olive oil are rich in fatty acids that directly feed Malassezia yeast, causing explosive outbreaks.
