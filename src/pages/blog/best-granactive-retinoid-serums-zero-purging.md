**Granactive retinoid** (hydroxypinacolone retinoate, or HPR) is a retinoid ester that binds to retinoid receptors directly, without the conversion steps retinol needs. That's why it's generally considered **gentler, with less dryness and purging**, though it is less studied than retinol or prescription retinoids.

It's a good starting point for sensitive skin or for anyone who couldn't tolerate retinol. Here's how the granactive option on Amazon India compares with classic retinol.
