Cracked heels happen when the thick skin on your heels gets **too dry and hard to stretch** as you walk. It is common in India because of open footwear, dust, standing for long hours and dry winters.

**The best foot creams combine:**
- **Urea (10–40%)**, which both moisturises and softens hard skin. Higher strengths soften more.
- **Lactic or salicylic acid**, which loosens thick, dead skin.
- **Occlusives**, which seal in moisture.

**A simple routine:**
1. Soak feet in warm water for 10 minutes a few times a week.
2. Gently use a pumice stone on hard skin. **Never cut calluses yourself.**
3. Apply foot cream at night and wear cotton socks.
4. Wear closed-back footwear when you can.

**If you have diabetes**, check your feet daily and see a doctor about any cracks, sores or infections rather than treating them at home. Deep, bleeding cracks should also be seen by a doctor. These are the foot creams we rate most highly.
