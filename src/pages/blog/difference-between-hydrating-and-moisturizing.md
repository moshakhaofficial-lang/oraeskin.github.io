**Hydrating** products add water to the skin using humectants like hyaluronic acid and glycerin. They help **dehydrated** skin, which can feel tight even when oily. **Moisturising** products use emollients and occlusives (ceramides, oils, butters) to seal water in and repair the barrier. They help **dry** skin, which lacks oil.

Most skin needs both: a hydrating layer first, then a moisturiser on top. Oily skin often needs only a light gel; dry skin needs a richer cream. These are the products on Amazon India we rate most highly for each role.
