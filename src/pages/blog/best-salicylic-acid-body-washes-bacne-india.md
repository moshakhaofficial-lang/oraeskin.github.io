Back and chest acne is common in India's heat and humidity, made worse by **sweat, tight clothing and backpacks**.

**Which wash to choose:**
- **Salicylic acid** washes are best for **clogged pores, blackheads and small bumps**.
- **Benzoyl peroxide** washes are better for **red, inflamed pimples**. They bleach fabric, so use white towels.

**How to use a body wash for acne:**
1. Lather onto the back and chest and **leave it on for 1–2 minutes** before rinsing.
2. Shower soon after sweating or workouts.
3. Wear loose, breathable cotton clothes.
4. Rinse conditioner off your back last, as it can clog pores.

Painful, deep or scarring body acne should be seen by a dermatologist. Itchy, uniform bumps that don't respond to acne washes may be fungal acne, which needs different treatment. These are the body washes we rate most highly.
