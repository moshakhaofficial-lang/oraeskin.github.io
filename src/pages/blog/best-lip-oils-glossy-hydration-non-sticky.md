Lip oils give a **glossy, juicy finish** that feels lighter than a traditional gloss. The best ones use **slip-y plant oils (jojoba, apricot, avocado)** or light synthetic oils, not thick, tacky polymers.

**Lip oil vs lip balm:** oils are mainly for shine and comfort. They do not seal in moisture as well as a wax-based balm, so **very dry or cracked lips still need a balm**, especially at night.

**Also worth knowing:**
- **Colour-changing oils** use dyes that react with your lips' moisture and pH to give a pink tint.
- Most lip oils contain **flavour or fragrance**. Skip them if your lips get irritated easily.
- None of these contain SPF, so use an SPF lip balm underneath when outdoors.

These are the lip oils we rate most highly.
