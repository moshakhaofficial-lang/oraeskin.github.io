## Editorial Routine Tips for Indian Skin

1. **Use 2 to 3 Times Weekly**: Never apply 8% Glycolic Acid daily to delicate underarms. Start twice weekly at night on clean, completely dry skin using [Minimalist 8% Glycolic Toner](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B0BNBXWVY3?tag=oraeskin-21).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Glycolic Acid on my face?
Yes, but 8% glycolic acid can be potent for sensitive facial skin. If using on the face, limit to once or twice a week at night and always use sunscreen during the day.

### Does Glycolic Acid eliminate body odor?
Yes! By acidifying the underarm surface to pH 3.5-4.0, glycolic acid inhibits odor-causing bacteria from metabolizing sweat into unpleasant carboxylic acids.
