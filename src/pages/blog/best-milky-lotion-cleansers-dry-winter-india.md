## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I skip washing my face in the morning during winter?
If you have extremely dry or sensitive skin, splashing with lukewarm water or using a non-foaming lotion cleanser like Cetaphil is ideal to protect your natural lipid shield.

### Why does my regular face wash suddenly sting in December?
Cold winds and indoor heaters deplete stratum corneum lipids, exposing microscopic nerve endings that sting when exposed to alkaline surfactants.
