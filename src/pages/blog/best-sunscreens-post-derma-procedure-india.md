## Frequently Asked Questions

### When can I start applying sunscreen after a chemical peel?
You should apply a gentle, fragrance-free ceramide sunscreen starting the very next morning after your clinic procedure.

### Why is Indian skin more prone to post-laser dark spots?
Higher baseline melanin concentrations mean melanocytes respond aggressively to any procedure-related inflammation by overproducing pigment (PIH). Strict sun protection is mandatory.
