In India's tropical climate, your facial cleanser is the most crucial step of your routine. Between particulate pollution (PM 2.5), high humidity, and long-lasting sunscreens, improper cleansing leads directly to dullness, blackheads, and inflammatory acne.

Choosing a pH-balanced cleanser (ideally between 4.5 and 5.5) protects the skin’s acid mantle, removing impurities without stripping natural moisture.

## The 60-Second Rule & Double Cleansing

Most people rinse their facewash after just 10 seconds, not giving active ingredients enough contact time to work.

1. **Massage for 60 Seconds**: Gently massage lather across your forehead, nose, and chin in circular motions for a full minute before rinsing with lukewarm water.
2. **Double Cleanse at Night**: If wearing waterproof sunscreen or makeup, start with micellar water or cleansing oil to break down silicones, then follow with your foaming wash.
3. **Always Follow with Sunscreen (AM)**: Clean skin is more permeable to sunlight. Defend your barrier daily with **[Minimalist SPF 50](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B09FPS9D5T?tag=oraeskin-21)**.
