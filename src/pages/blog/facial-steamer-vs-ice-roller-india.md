Facial steamers and ice rollers are both bestsellers on Amazon India, and they do nearly opposite things. One heats your skin and the other cools it. So which should you use?

**Facial steaming:**
- **What it does:** warm, moist air softens the top layer of skin and feels relaxing.
- **What it doesn't do:** pores don't "open" and close like doors. Steam doesn't deep-clean them.
- **Who should avoid it:** anyone with **melasma, rosacea, eczema or sensitive skin**. Heat can worsen redness and pigmentation, and frequent steaming can dry skin out.

**Ice rolling:**
- **What it does:** cold briefly narrows blood vessels, which reduces puffiness and redness for a few hours.
- **What it doesn't do:** it doesn't shrink pores permanently or change your skin long term.
- **Who should avoid it:** people whose skin reacts badly to cold. Never hold ice in one spot.

**Our verdict:** for most Indian skin, especially in hot weather or with pigmentation, **cold tools are the safer daily choice**. Keep steaming as an occasional treat, at most once a week. We compared the most popular options and scored each with our [review method](/how-we-review/).

## How to Use Each Safely

**Ice roller:** wrap it or let it sit for 30 seconds after taking it out of the freezer. Keep it moving for 1–2 minutes, and never hold it on one spot. Clean it after each use.

**Steamer:** keep your face at least 30 cm away, steam for 5 minutes at most, no more than once a week, then moisturise straight away. Skip it entirely if you have melasma, rosacea or eczema, or after retinoids or chemical peels.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does steam open pores?
No. Pores don't have muscles to open or close. Steam softens skin and can make oil feel looser, but it doesn't clean pores. For blackheads, [salicylic acid](/blog/best-salicylic-acid-cleansers-blackheads-pores/) works much better.

### Is ice rolling good for acne?
Cold can briefly calm the redness and swelling of an inflamed pimple. Use a clean roller, never one you have used on other skin, and don't roll over broken spots.

### Is steaming bad for melasma?
Heat is a known trigger for melasma and pigmentation, so we advise people with melasma to avoid facial steaming.
