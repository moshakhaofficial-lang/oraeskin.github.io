When you start a retinoid, faster skin-cell turnover can bring existing clogs to the surface. That's **purging**. It usually shows up as small whiteheads or bumps **in areas where you normally break out**, starts within the first 2–3 weeks, and settles within 4–8 weeks.

A **breakout or reaction** is different: spots appear in **new areas**, last beyond 8–10 weeks, or come with burning, itching or rash-like redness. That points to irritation or a product that doesn't suit you.

To reduce purging, start low, use your retinoid 2–3 nights a week, buffer it with moisturiser, and don't add other exfoliants. These are the beginner-friendly retinoids and support products on Amazon India we rate most highly.
