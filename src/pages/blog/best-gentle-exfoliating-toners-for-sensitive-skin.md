## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between AHA, BHA, and PHA?
AHAs (Glycolic) are water-soluble small molecules for surface exfoliation; BHAs (Salicylic) are oil-soluble for pore cleaning; PHAs (Gluconolactone) are large molecules that gently exfoliate the surface while deeply hydrating.

### Can I use PHA toner in the summer?
Yes! PHAs do not increase skin photosensitivity to UV rays as much as traditional AHAs, making them ideal year-round exfoliators for tropical Indian climates.
