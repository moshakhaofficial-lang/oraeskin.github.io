Cold, dry air pulls water out of your skin, and hot showers strip the oils that hold it in. That is why legs and arms turn flaky and itchy from November to February, especially in North India and in air-conditioned offices.

**A body lotion fixes this in two ways:**
- **Humectants** such as glycerin pull water into the outer layer of skin.
- **Occlusives and emollients** such as petroleum jelly, mineral oil, shea butter and cocoa butter stop that water escaping.

A good winter lotion has both, high up in its ingredient list. Claims like "72-hour moisture" matter far less than what is actually in the bottle.

When we checked Amazon India's skin care bestseller list in mid-November 2026, several of the top 30 products were body lotions. We read the ingredient list of five of the most popular and scored them with our [formulation-based method](/how-we-review/).

## How to Get More From Any Body Lotion

- **Apply within three minutes of bathing**, on slightly damp skin. This traps the water already on your skin.
- **Keep showers warm, not hot**, and short. Very hot water removes skin oils.
- **Use about a ₹10-coin amount per limb** and reapply to hands, shins and elbows at night.
- **Switch to a gentler body wash** if your skin feels tight after bathing.

**For rough bumps or scaly patches**, a plain lotion may not be enough. A urea or lactic acid lotion softens that build-up; see our guides to [urea lotions for strawberry legs](/blog/best-urea-lotions-strawberry-legs-keratosis-pilaris/) and [lactic acid lotions for rough elbows and knees](/blog/best-lactic-acid-lotions-rough-elbows-knees-india/). See a dermatologist if skin cracks, bleeds or itches enough to disturb your sleep.
