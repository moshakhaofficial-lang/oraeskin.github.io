Vitamin C serums are one of the most-bought skin care products in India, and three of the bestsellers on Amazon cost under ₹350. Cheaper doesn't have to mean worse, but there are two things to check.

**1. The form of vitamin C.** Pure L-ascorbic acid is the most studied but goes off quickly in Indian heat, turning yellow or orange. **3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid** (ethyl ascorbic acid) is far more stable and is a good choice for budget serums.

**2. Whether the strength is stated.** A label that says "10% ethyl ascorbic acid" tells you what you are getting. "Vitamin C" with no form or percentage doesn't.

**What to expect:** a brighter look and gradual fading of acne marks and dark spots over 8–12 weeks, used every morning under sunscreen. We compared three serums under ₹350 and scored each with our [formulation-based method](/how-we-review/).

## How to Use a Vitamin C Serum

1. **Morning, on clean, dry skin.** Use 3–4 drops for the face and neck.
2. **Follow with moisturiser and sunscreen.** Vitamin C supports sunscreen but never replaces it.
3. **Store it away from heat and sunlight.** Keep the cap closed tightly.
4. **If you also use niacinamide**, it's fine to layer them. The old advice to keep them apart doesn't apply to these modern formulas.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a ₹200 vitamin C serum as good as a ₹600 one?
It can be, if it states a stable vitamin C at a useful strength. Both [Pilgrim](/reviews/pilgrim-10-vitc-serum/) and [Chemist At Play](/reviews/chemist-at-play-10-vitc-serum/) list 10% ethyl ascorbic acid, the same form and strength as many pricier serums. The main differences are bottle size and extra ingredients.

### Which is the best value?
[Garnier](/reviews/garnier-vitamin-c-serum/) is cheapest at about ₹10 per ml, but its strength isn't stated. Of the two with stated strengths, [Pilgrim](/reviews/pilgrim-10-vitc-serum/) is slightly cheaper at about ₹21 per ml. Prices were checked on Amazon.in on 22 November 2026 and change often.

### My vitamin C serum turned yellow. Is it still OK?
Serums with ethyl ascorbic acid are much less likely to change colour. If any serum turns dark orange or brown, it has oxidised and is less effective, so replace it.

### Can sensitive skin use vitamin C?
Usually, yes, with ethyl ascorbic acid, which is gentler than pure L-ascorbic acid. Start every other morning.
