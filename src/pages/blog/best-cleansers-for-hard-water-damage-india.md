## Frequently Asked Questions

### How does hard water cause acne?
Calcium and magnesium ions react with natural fatty acids in sebum to form insoluble mineral salts that physically plug follicular openings, creating breeding grounds for acne bacteria.

### Can a shower filter fix hard water for facial washing?
Shower filters remove chlorine and sediment, but they cannot soften water or remove dissolved calcium without expensive ion-exchange water softeners. Chelating cleansers are the most practical solution.
