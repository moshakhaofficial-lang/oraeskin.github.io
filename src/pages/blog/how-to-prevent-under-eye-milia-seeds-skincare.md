**Milia** are tiny, hard white cysts of trapped keratin, common under the eyes. They aren't pimples and don't pop. Squeezing them damages delicate skin. Heavy, occlusive eye creams can contribute, as can sun damage and skipping gentle exfoliation.

**To prevent them:** switch to lightweight eye products, use a gentle retinoid to support cell turnover, and wear sunscreen. **To remove existing milia,** see a dermatologist, who can extract them safely.
