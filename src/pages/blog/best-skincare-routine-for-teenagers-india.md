## Frequently Asked Questions

### At what age can a teenager start using skincare?
A basic routine (gentle cleanser, light moisturizer, sunscreen) can be started around age 12-13. Active ingredients like 2% Salicylic acid can be introduced when pubertal blackheads appear.

### Can teenagers use Retinol?
No. Unless prescribed by a dermatologist for severe cystic acne, teenagers should avoid anti-aging retinoids and stick to Salicylic acid and Niacinamide.
