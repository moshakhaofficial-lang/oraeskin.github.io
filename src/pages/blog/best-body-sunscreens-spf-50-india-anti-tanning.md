Arms, neck, hands and feet get as much sun as your face during commutes, but are often left unprotected. That is why **tan lines and uneven skin tone** build up on them.

**How to use body sunscreen properly:**
- **Use enough.** Covering the whole body takes about 30ml (a shot glass). Each arm needs about two finger-lengths.
- Apply **15 minutes before going out**.
- **Reapply every 2–3 hours** outdoors, and after sweating heavily or swimming.
- Choose **SPF 50 with PA++++** or named UVA filters for tanning protection.

None of these picks are labelled water-resistant, so reapply after swimming. Covering up with clothing, sleeves and a hat is also very effective. These are the body sunscreens we rate most highly.
