## Eye Care Tips for Contact Lens Wearers

1. **Apply Eye Cream AFTER Inserting Lenses**: Insert your contact lenses first with clean, dry hands; then apply [The Ordinary Caffeine Solution 5% + EGCG](https://www.amazon.in/dp/B01MUZVE1C?tag=oraeskin-21) to the orbital bone.
2. **Stay Along the Orbital Bone**: Never apply creams directly onto the water line or eyelid margins. Tap gently along the cheekbone ridge.
3. **Remove Lenses Before PM Cleansing**: Take out your lenses before using cleansers or eye makeup removers at night.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my eye cream make my vision blurry?
Heavy oils in creams melt at body temperature and migrate into your tear film, forming a lipid layer over your contact lens that disperses light and causes haziness.

### Can eye creams cause eye infections for lens wearers?
Yes, if they contain non-ophthalmic synthetic fragrances or if fingers contaminate the jar. Always use tube packaging and wash hands thoroughly.
