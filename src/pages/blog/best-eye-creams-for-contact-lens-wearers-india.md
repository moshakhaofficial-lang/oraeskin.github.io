Tired of eye creams migrating into your eyes, stinging, and blurring your contact lenses? Discover ophthalmologist-tested, non-creeping periorbital treatments.

Millions of people in India wear soft or rigid gas-permeable contact lenses daily. However, many conventional under-eye creams contain fluid oils and volatile silicones that slowly 'creep' via capillary action across the lower lid margin into the tear film. When lipids coat contact lenses, they cause clouding, blurry vision, stinging, and protein deposit buildup. Lens wearers require biomimetic, non-migrating formulations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my eye cream make my vision blurry?
Heavy oils in creams melt at body temperature and migrate into your tear film, forming a lipid layer over your contact lens that disperses light and causes haziness.

### Can eye creams cause eye infections for lens wearers?
Yes, if they contain non-ophthalmic synthetic fragrances or if fingers contaminate the jar. Always use tube packaging and wash hands thoroughly.
