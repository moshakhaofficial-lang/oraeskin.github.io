Men's skin is usually oilier than women's and has larger pores, and daily shaving adds irritation. In Indian cities, add sweat, helmets and traffic pollution, and most men end up with shiny skin, blackheads and the odd breakout.

**The good news:** skin doesn't care whose name is on the bottle. A "men's" face wash is usually a regular wash with a stronger scent and a cooling feel. What matters is the ingredients:
- **Salicylic acid** clears oil from inside pores and has strong evidence for blackheads and acne.
- **Clay and charcoal** soak up surface oil and grime.
- **A soap-free formula** avoids the tight, dry feel that makes skin produce even more oil.

## A Simple Routine for Men

1. **Morning:** wash with one of the face washes above, then apply sunscreen. See our [best sunscreens for men in India](/blog/best-sunscreen-for-men-in-india/).
2. **Night:** wash again to remove sweat, oil and pollution, then use a light moisturiser.
3. **Shaving:** shave after washing, with the grain, and use a soothing moisturiser after. Our [aftershave moisturizers for razor bumps](/blog/best-aftershave-moisturizers-razor-bumps-india/) guide covers this.
4. **Post-workout:** rinse sweat off within an hour. See [post-workout face washes for sweat acne](/blog/best-post-workout-face-washes-sweat-acne/).

**Wash twice a day, no more.** Washing more often strips your skin, and it may respond by making more oil.
