**Polyhydroxy acids (PHAs)**, such as gluconolactone and lactobionic acid, exfoliate like AHAs but have larger molecules. They penetrate more slowly, so they usually cause **less stinging and redness**, and they also attract water.

**Who they suit:** sensitive, reactive or dry skin that finds glycolic acid too strong, and anyone new to exfoliation. The trade-off is that results are gentler and slower than with glycolic or salicylic acid.

Even with a gentle acid, stop if your skin burns or stays red. A compromised barrier needs rest and moisturiser first. These are the PHA products on Amazon India we rate most highly, plus a hydrating toner to pair with them.
