## How to Choose a Face Wash for Oily Skin

| Your skin | Choose | Our pick |

| Oily with blackheads and whiteheads | Salicylic acid 1–2% | [Minimalist 2%](/reviews/minimalist-salicylic-cleanser/) or [Derma Co 1%](/reviews/dermaco-1-salicylic-facewash/) |
| Oily with red, painful pimples | Benzoyl peroxide 2.5% | [Derma Co 2.5% BPO](/reviews/dermaco-bpo-facewash/) |
| Oily and sensitive or easily red | Gentle, fragrance-free foam | [CeraVe Foaming](/reviews/cerave-foaming-cleanser/) or [Cetaphil Oily Skin](/reviews/cetaphil-oily-skin-cleanser/) |
| Already using a retinoid or acne cream | Acid-free wash | [La Roche-Posay Effaclar](/reviews/lrp-effaclar-purifying-gel/) |
| Just shiny, no acne | Zinc or plain gel wash | [Re'equil Oil Control](/reviews/reequil-oil-control-facewash/) |

**Foaming or gel?** Both suit oily skin. What matters is that your skin feels clean but not tight afterwards. If it feels squeaky or stings, switch to a gentler wash.

## Morning vs Night: How Often to Wash

1. **Twice a day is enough**, even in humid weather. Washing three or four times strips skin and can make it feel oilier.
2. **Morning:** a gentle wash such as CeraVe, Cetaphil or Re'equil.
3. **Night:** your active wash (salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide) to remove sunscreen, oil and pollution. Wearing heavy sunscreen? Read [how to remove sunscreen properly](/blog/how-to-remove-sunscreen-properly-double-cleansing/).
4. **Massage for 30–60 seconds**, rinse with lukewarm water, pat dry, and use a light moisturiser such as [Minimalist Vitamin B5](/reviews/minimalist-b5-moisturizer/).
5. **After the gym**, a quick wash with your gentle cleanser is fine.

A face wash is only on your skin for a minute. For stubborn acne, add a leave-on treatment, such as a [salicylic acid serum](/blog/best-salicylic-acid-serums-acne-whiteheads-india/) or [spot gel](/blog/best-salicylic-acid-spot-gels-urgent-breakouts/), and see a dermatologist if acne is painful or scarring.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which is the best face wash for oily skin and pimples?
A salicylic acid wash. The [Minimalist 2% Salicylic Acid Face Wash](/reviews/minimalist-salicylic-cleanser/) is our top pick, and [The Derma Co 1%](/reviews/dermaco-1-salicylic-facewash/) is gentler if you're new to acids. For red, painful pimples, use [benzoyl peroxide](/reviews/dermaco-bpo-facewash/) instead.

### What is the best cleanser for oily skin in India if I don't have acne?
A gentle foam such as [CeraVe Foaming Cleanser](/reviews/cerave-foaming-cleanser/), or [Re'equil Oil Control](/reviews/reequil-oil-control-facewash/) on a budget. You don't need acids if you only want to control shine.

### Is a foaming cleanser good for oily skin?
Yes, as long as it's soap-free and doesn't leave your skin tight. All eight picks here are suitable for oily skin.

### Can I use a salicylic acid face wash every day?
1% is fine twice a day for most oily skin. With 2%, start once a day at night and increase only if your skin stays comfortable.

### Salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide?
Salicylic acid is better for blackheads, whiteheads and oily pores. Benzoyl peroxide is better for red, inflamed pimples. Don't use both washes on the same day when you're starting out.
