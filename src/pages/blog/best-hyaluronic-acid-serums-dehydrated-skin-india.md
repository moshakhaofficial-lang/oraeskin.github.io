Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a **humectant**: it holds water in the top layers of skin, which makes skin look plumper and feel less tight. Serums that mix several molecular weights hydrate at slightly different depths.

**Why HA sometimes feels drying:** in dry air or air-conditioned rooms, a humectant has little water to draw from. Apply HA on **damp skin**, then seal it with a moisturiser. On its own, it is not a moisturiser.

**Dehydrated is not the same as dry.** Even oily skin can be dehydrated, which shows up as tightness, dullness and fine lines alongside shine. HA suits every skin type. These are the HA serums on Amazon India we rate most highly.
