## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do pimple patches work on deep cystic acne?
Hydrocolloid patches work best on pimples that have come to a head (whiteheads/pustules). For deep, blind cystic bumps, a topical gel like Sebogel or a warm compress is more effective.

### Can I put makeup over a pimple patch?
Yes! High-grade hydrocolloid patches feature matte beveled edges that allow foundation or concealer to sit smoothly on top while keeping the lesion sterile.
