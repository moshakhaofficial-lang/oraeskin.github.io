Lips have very little melanin of their own and almost no protective outer layer, so they **burn and darken easily in the sun**. Sun exposure is also a common cause of darker, uneven lips in India.

A **tinted lip balm with SPF** gives you sun protection and a little colour in one step, which is ideal for college or office.

**What to look for:**
- **SPF 30 or higher**, ideally SPF 50 with PA ratings or named UVA filters.
- A sheer tint close to your natural lip colour for everyday wear.

**Reapply every 2 hours outdoors, and after eating or drinking**, since lip SPF wears off quickly. Brightening ingredients like kojic acid or vitamin C help a little, but **daily SPF does most of the work** in preventing lip darkening. These are the tinted SPF lip balms we rate most highly.
