"De-tan" face washes fill Amazon India's face care bestseller list. When we checked it on 19 November 2026, six of them were in the top 80. They promise to remove tan, but it helps to know what a face wash can realistically do.

**What a face wash can do for a tan:**
- **Exfoliating acids** such as lactic, glycolic and salicylic acid, and enzymes such as papain, loosen the dull, tanned top layer of skin, so your face looks brighter sooner.
- **Ingredients like niacinamide, vitamin C and kojic acid** work on pigment, but a face wash is rinsed off within a minute, so they have little time to act.
- **A gentle formula matters most.** Stripping your skin twice a day causes irritation, which can make dark patches worse on Indian skin.

**What actually fades a tan:** daily sunscreen, so no new tan forms, and time. A tan fades on its own over a few weeks once you protect your skin. A de-tan wash can speed up the brighter look a little. For stubborn dark spots, a leave-on serum works far better than any wash.

## How to Fade a Tan Faster

1. **Wear sunscreen every morning**, including on cloudy days, and reapply if you are outdoors. See our [best sunscreens for Indian skin](/blog/best-sunscreens-for-indian-skin/).
2. **Wash gently, twice a day at most.** Rubbing hard or scrubbing irritates skin and can make it darker.
3. **Add a leave-on brightening serum at night** if the tan or spots linger. [Niacinamide](/blog/best-niacinamide-serums-open-pores-india/) and [vitamin C](/blog/best-vitamin-c-serums-for-indian-skin/) serums stay on your skin long enough to work.
4. **Moisturise after washing**, especially with the soap-based washes above.

**See a dermatologist** if dark patches don't fade after three months of sunscreen. That can be melasma, which needs different treatment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a face wash really remove a tan?
Not on its own. Exfoliating ingredients can make tanned skin look brighter a little sooner, but the tan fades mainly with sun protection and time. Expect a gradual change over two to six weeks, not overnight results.

### What is triclosan, and should I avoid it?
Triclosan is an antibacterial ingredient. The US and EU have restricted it in many consumer washes because there is little evidence it helps more than plain soap, along with ongoing questions about its safety. Both DERMATOUCH washes contain it. If you would rather avoid it, choose another pick from this list.
