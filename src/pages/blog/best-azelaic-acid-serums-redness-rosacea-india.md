Azelaic acid is unusual because it works on three problems at once: it is **anti-inflammatory** (calms redness), **antibacterial** (helps acne) and **slows melanin production** (fades marks). It is also considered one of the safer actives during pregnancy, though you should confirm with your doctor.

**Strength matters.** Over-the-counter serums usually contain about **10%**. Prescription creams and gels (15–20%) are stronger, so if you have **diagnosed rosacea**, see a dermatologist rather than self-treating.

**How to start:** use it every other night for two weeks. Mild tingling at first is common. These are the azelaic acid options on Amazon India we rate most highly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long before I see noticeable results?
Hydrating and soothing products feel better within days. Acne actives usually need 4–8 weeks, and pigment or anti-ageing actives 8–12 weeks of consistent use alongside daily sunscreen.

### Will this cause breakouts in humid weather?
Most picks above are light formulas, but any product can clog pores for some people. Introduce one new product at a time and give it a week before adding the next, especially if your skin is acne-prone.
