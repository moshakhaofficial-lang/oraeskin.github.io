## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do inner thighs turn dark from chafing?
Chronic friction causes mechanical micro-trauma. Melanocytes in South Asian skin respond to this chronic trauma by producing extra melanin as a defense mechanism, causing dark patches.

### Can baby powder prevent chafing?
Talcum and cornstarch powders absorb sweat initially, but they quickly clump into an abrasive paste that actually worsens friction. Cream-based barrier balms are far superior.
