Ice rollers, gua sha stones, face brushes and patches fill Amazon India's skin care tools bestseller list. Some are genuinely useful. Some are pleasant but optional. A few can harm your skin.

**What tools can and can't do:**
- **Cold tools** (ice rollers, gel eye masks) reduce puffiness for a few hours.
- **Massage tools** (gua sha) feel relaxing and can briefly de-puff. There is little evidence they "lift" or reshape the face.
- **Cleansing tools** (silicone brushes) help spread cleanser and remove sunscreen, but clean fingers work almost as well.
- **Hydrocolloid patches** have real evidence for helping surfaced pimples heal and stopping you from picking.

None of them replace sunscreen, a gentle cleanser and a moisturiser. We looked at the most popular tools on Amazon India and scored each with our [review method](/how-we-review/).

## Tools We Don't Recommend Buying

These sell well on Amazon India, but they can do more harm than good at home. We don't link to them.

- **Derma rollers and derma stamps for the face.** At-home microneedling can cause infection, scarring and dark marks (post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation), which is a particular risk for Indian skin. Rollers also blunt and bend with use. If you want microneedling for acne scars, get it done by a dermatologist with sterile, single-use needles.
- **Electric blackhead vacuums.** Strong suction can break small blood vessels and leave bruises, and it doesn't stop blackheads coming back.
- **Metal blackhead and pimple extractor kits.** Squeezing at home pushes bacteria deeper and causes marks and scars.

For blackheads, a [salicylic acid cleanser](/blog/best-salicylic-acid-cleansers-blackheads-pores/) works better over time. Our guide [do nose strips work?](/blog/do-nose-strips-work-best-nose-strips-india/) explains the quick-fix option.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which skincare tool is most worth buying?
[Hydrocolloid pimple patches](/reviews/mighty-patch-pimple-patches/) have the best evidence and stop you picking at spots. After that, a [cooling eye mask](/reviews/boldhealth-cooling-eye-gel-mask/) is the most useful for everyday puffiness.

### Does gua sha really lift the face?
There is little evidence it lifts or reshapes the face over time. It can reduce morning puffiness for a few hours and feels relaxing. Use it gently with oil, a few times a week.

### Is a face brush better than my hands?
For most people, not by much. A silicone brush helps spread cleanser evenly and can help remove heavy sunscreen, but hands are gentler on sensitive or acne-prone skin.

### How do I keep tools clean?
Wash silicone and steel tools with mild soap after each use, let them dry fully, and don't share them. Replace cotton pads and patches after one use.
