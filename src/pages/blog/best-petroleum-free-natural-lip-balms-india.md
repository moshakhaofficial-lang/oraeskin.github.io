Many classic lip balms are based on **petrolatum (petroleum jelly), mineral oil or paraffin**. These are safe and very effective at sealing in moisture, so "petroleum-free" is a preference, not a safety issue.

If you would rather use plant-based balms, look for **plant butters (shea, cocoa, kokum), plant oils (castor, jojoba, olive) and plant waxes (carnauba, candelilla)**.

**Vegan is not the same as natural.** Many natural balms use **beeswax** or honey, so check the label if you are vegan. We have marked which picks contain them.

Natural balms often include **essential oils or flavour**, which can irritate sensitive lips. These are the petroleum-free balms we rate most highly.
