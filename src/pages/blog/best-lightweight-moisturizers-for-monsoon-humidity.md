## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need moisturizer during monsoon if my face feels oily?
Yes! When skin is stripped of moisture by humidity-induced washing, it produces excess oil to compensate. A water-based oil-free moisturizer balances this hydration-oil equilibrium.

### Can humidity cause fungal acne?
Yes, high heat combined with sweat fosters Malassezia yeast proliferation. Avoid heavy esters, fatty acids, and oils during monsoon season.
